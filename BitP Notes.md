Ok. So.

When an operation begins you become entangled with the worldliness of the other participants. Until it is finished no one can regain spent Span by resting. The GM has final say on what qualifies as being away enough to disentangle. (This is basically the same as the time combat rule in CONTINUUM)

Spanning actions:

You may at any time span levelly. Teleporting up to 1 mile in any direction without changing time. This is instantaneous and costs nothing. You do not need to be able to see the destination, but spanning into a location you can't see may require a fortune roll to avoid unexpected complications (like being seen).

You may also at any time take a tactical span action to span either Up or Down by up to one week (and also up to one mile in any direction in the same action). This costs one tick of your tactical spanning clocks (5 x 10 segment clocks).

If you are in a stable position and out of any danger you may tick multiple segments to span multiple weeks in one go.

In either case record your start time and end time of your span on your record sheet.


If you are in a safe and stable situation at any time you may choose to kill time. This allows you to move Up by any length of time of your choosing for the cost of no span segments. Record it. You may immediately take one downtime action per full week you killed time for.

When not entangled (eg. in free play or downtime) your full allotment of spanning segments are refreshed every time you sleep a full night's sleep. If you have your entire allotment of segments unused you may spend them all at once to span 1 year in either direction.

The alienation track: every character has a 12 segment alienation track. Each time you kill time tick off one segment per week killed. When the clock fills take a minor mental harm.



Support: every character has a support, a group of friends, relatives, pets, or even just routines that help them recover from alienation. When killing time in the same locality as your support you take no alienation and instead remove one point of alienation for each week killed.



Starting characters' support will always consist of levellers, so avoiding alienation requires living life levelly.



As characters develop they will gain alternate means of avoiding or reducing alienation, and may gain additional or replacement supports.







The total cost of killing time for downtime actions: usually 1 span segment and 1 alienation per downtime action, assuming the PC wishes to return to the exact moment they spanned away from, and that their support is not in the same locality they are currently in. The span segment cost can be avoided if the PC is willing to continue the action further Up rather than spanning back.



If an action can only be taken at a specific locality then the PC must span to that locality first, and may need to span back from it afterwards.





Spending Frag:



There are many ways to take Frag as a result of others' actions, but the ways you can specifically chose to gain Frag to achieve an effect are important:



* Declare a detail: you may declare any single detail of the situation not directly in contravention of established fact. This will usually cost 1 Frag, though for details which would be particularly hard to arrange it may cost more. The Frag gained this way can be cleared only by arranging the detail which caused the Frag.
* Beneficial situation: On any die roll you may declare that you have a beneficial situation. This costs 2 Frag and gets you +1d on the roll. To clear this Frag you must set up the situation which caused it.
* Avoid consequences: any time you are about to suffer a consequence you may reduce its severity by one level by declaring a helpful detail which reduces the consequence. When you do this roll dice equal to one of your three attributes, as appropriate to the kind of consequence. The Frag cost is 6-the highest value rolled. This Frag can be cleared by arranging the helpful detail.
* Gemini gambit: For a cost of one Frag and one Gemini bring a Elder to your current moment. This Frag can be cleared only by travelling to this moment and performing the same actions the Elder was observed to perform.

Whenever you take Frag record the details of how to clear it in your Yet log.



Most Yet entries can be cleared by travelling to the appropriate locality and spending a number of downtime actions equal to the number of Frag to clear. A few may require, at GM's discretion, a more involved solution, up to and including an entire operation.

## Antiquarians

**Seakers of Artifacts**

Your Corner is connected to the antiquarian fraternity. You may not all be actual inducted members yet, but the corner as a whole leans that way, and your Mentor is almost certainly a member in good standing. 

Your corner collects historical artifacts. Unique and important items. Some of them are made of gold and covered in precious gems, but many are not, the importance is how much they matter to history. And such items usually have well documented histories. Which makes acquiring them tricky. You can't just span to a time and place where they were located and then steal them: that's a great way to Frag your entire Corner!

To do it properly you need to work out somewhere the artifact won't be missed from: gaps in their history, points just before destruction, or times to which they can be returned near instantaneously after being taken. 

*As Antiquarians mark Corner XP every time you execute a successful Burglary, Acquisition, or Purchase*
